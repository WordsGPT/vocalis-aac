The numbered PNG files in this directory are unmodified 300px ARASAAC pictograms.
Each filename is the ARASAAC pictogram identifier. Original files can be found at
https://static.arasaac.org/pictograms/{id}/{id}_300.png .

This license applies to the pictogram assets, independently of the application's code.
The application displays the attribution in its board and settings.
